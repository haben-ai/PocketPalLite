## Full description (max 4000 characters — works for both Google Play and Apple App Store)

**Zayla is a private AI chat companion that runs entirely on your phone — no internet connection required, no account, no data collection, ever.**

Most AI chat apps send every message you type to a server somewhere. Zayla doesn't. Once you've downloaded a model, your conversations are processed and stored only on your device. There's no backend to talk to, nothing to leak, and nothing to subscribe to.

**Choose the right model for your device**
Zayla analyzes your phone's hardware once and recommends a model that actually fits — no more guessing whether a model will run smoothly or drain your battery. Pick from a curated catalog of small, capable open models (Google Gemma, Microsoft Phi, and more), or search Hugging Face directly to download any GGUF model you want.

**Real AI, real control**
This isn't a toy chatbot. Zayla exposes the same generation controls serious users care about — temperature, top-k/top-p, repetition penalties, Mirostat, custom seeds — so you can tune how a model responds instead of accepting one fixed personality. A built-in benchmark tool measures real generation speed on your actual device, not a marketing number.

**AIPals: build your own assistants**
Create custom personas with their own name, avatar, and system prompt. Switch between a coding helper, a writing partner, or a study buddy without losing context between them.

**Vision support**
Some models understand images too — attach a photo and ask about it, all still processed locally.

**Built for offline life**
Download once over Wi-Fi, then use it anywhere: on a plane, underground, in a place with no signal, or just somewhere you don't want your phone constantly phoning home. Zayla checks your connection before starting a download so it fails clearly instead of silently, and resumes cleanly if it's interrupted.

**No accounts. No ads. No tracking.**
There's nothing to sign up for and nothing collecting analytics on how you use it. The only thing Zayla downloads is the model you choose — everything after that happens on your device.

**Also included**
- Export and import conversations
- On-device translation
- Light and dark themes
- Import your own local GGUF models

Zayla is for anyone who wants a genuinely private, capable AI assistant — without wondering where their conversations end up.

## Notes for whoever fills in the store listing forms

- Both Google Play and Apple App Store cap the full description at 4000 characters — the draft above is ~2050, so there's headroom to add localized keywords or a feature they specifically want highlighted.
- Google Play's short description (80 chars) shows directly under the app name in search results — it's the highest-leverage 80 characters in the whole listing.
- Apple doesn't have a "short description" field; the closest equivalents are the 30-character Subtitle (shown under the app name) and the 170-character Promotional Text (shown above the description, editable anytime without a new build/review).
- Screenshots for both stores should be captured directly from a real device at the target resolution (see screenshots/ folder) — store guidelines require the actual app UI, not mockups with fabricated content.
